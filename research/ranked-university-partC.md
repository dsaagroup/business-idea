## 1. PRIME STeP — ITB, IPB, UI, UGM (ADB-funded)

**Programme structure:** Promoting Research and Innovation through Modern and Efficient Science and Technology Parks, ADB Loan 4272-INO, 2023–2027, at IPB, ITB, UGM, UI. TRL 7–9 research-based startups; staged incubation → pre-acceleration → acceleration; 12–16 weeks.
**Rank finding (all cycles): cohort/no-rank.** No search result in this pass or the prior pass named an overall "winner", "best startup", 1st/2nd/3rd place, or demo-day award for any PRIME STeP cycle at any university. The published outcome per cycle is a selected/funded cohort. The Hackathon student track (IPB) is the one judged sub-event with grant recipients — see 2025/2026 rows; even there, recipients are a pass list (each Rp35m in 2025, Rp50m in 2026), **not** a ranked 1st/2nd/3rd.
**2022: no cycle.** Project introduced 27 Feb 2023 (2023–2027). Verified in prior pass; re-confirmed by the 2025 launch article calling 2025 "the third year" and the programme "targeted to run until 2027".
