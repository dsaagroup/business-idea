# Ranked Winners — TINC & Grab Ventures Velocity (Corporate & Bank, Group B)

Research date: 2026-11-15. Deep-research follow-up to `winners-corporate-bank.md` sections 5–6.
Scope: TINC Batches 7–11; GVV Batches 4–9 (incl. re-verification of previously found Batches 7–8 / Batch 9).

**Headline finding: neither programme publishes a ranked 1st/2nd/3rd winner in any cycle.**
Both are cohort / Demo Day accelerators: they announce selected cohorts/finalists, run PoCs/pilots,
and graduate them without ranking. No cycle below has a verified 1st, 2nd or 3rd place — and none
is invented here. Where a source spotlights individual startups (e.g. TINC Batch X "two leading
startups"), that is recorded as a spotlight, not a rank.

Prize note for both programmes: no cash prize per place was verified in any cycle. TINC is
cost-and-equity-free (PoC/collaboration, no stated prize); GVV states explicitly it provides
no direct funding — mentorship, Grab pilot, investor pitching only.

## 2. Grab Ventures Velocity (GVV) — Grab Indonesia + Endeavor Indonesia

Programme: accelerator launched 2018, annual, 12–18 weeks, post-seed Indonesian startups.
No direct funding (Grab states this explicitly); mentorship, Grab-ecosystem pilot, investor
pitching. Partners vary by batch: Batches 4–5 were joint with BRI Ventures' Sembrani Wira;
Batch 8 supported by Superbank + Genesis Alternative Ventures; Batch 9 supported by Superbank
+ Komdigi. Implemented by Endeavor Indonesia (Batches 8–9 confirmed).

Batch 4 launch announced Jun 2021, while Batch 3's cohort was announced Dec 2021
(Luna POS, KliknClean, Printerous, GetCraft, Workmate — KrASIA, 3 Dec 2021). Do not use
announcement dates to re-date batches; years below are the launch/announcement years as
