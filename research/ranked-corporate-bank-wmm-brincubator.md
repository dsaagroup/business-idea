## B. BRIncubator (Bank Rakyat Indonesia, Rumah BUMN)

### B.0 Structural finding (strengthened this round)

BRIncubator is **not one annual national competition**. Evidence:

1. **National (early) format:** launch edition (2018) and Go Global 2020 were national,
   with a ranked Top 3 + Top 10/25 finalists (see B.1, B.2).
2. **Local rolling format (2023–2025):** each Rumah BUMN hub runs its own
   "BRIncubator Lokal" — typically 1–2 months of training, then a local awarding with
   Juara 1/2/3 per hub (and per category within the hub). Makassar hub states the
   programme "digelar setiap empat bulan" (every four months), categories Food &
   Beverage, Fashion & Beauty, Home Décor & Craft.
   Source: https://gosulsel.com/05/2026/bri-dorong-inovasi-sarabba-instan-dari-sukma-jahe-semakin-terkenal/
3. **No national winner list is published** for 2021–2025 in any source found. BRI
   corporate press instead profiles individual winners (Netaly, Findmeera, Sisi).
   A "national 1st/2nd/3rd" for 2021–2025 therefore **does not exist as a published
   artefact** — the correct unit is hub × category × cycle.
4. **Do NOT confuse with Pengusaha Muda BRILiaN (PMB)** — a separate BRI programme
   (young entrepreneurs, 5 categories, national grand final, Top 150 → Top 75 → Top 20).
   PMB winners are NOT BRIncubator winners. PMB 2025: Sanfood Brilian Indonesia
   (F&B Best of the Best), Faber Instrument Indonesia (Home Decor & Craft),
   Hody (Favourite). PMB 2024: DBFOODS, Gulalibooks, Long Story Short, Bumbi,
   Mambucha, Supergoat (Favourite). PMB 2022: KaIND (1st), Nana Baby Carrier
   (runner-up), Ppuff! (3rd).
   Sources: https://youngontop.com/bri-umumkan-juara-pengusaha-muda-brilian-2025-dorong-entrepreneur-muda-naik-kelas-dan-unggul-di-pasar/ ;
   https://kombi.id/read/2026/02/13/bri-resmi-umumkan-juara-pengusaha-muda-brilian-2025-perkuat-komitmen-dukung-entrepreneur-muda-dan-umkm-naik-kelas/ ;
   https://youngontop.com/bergengsi-dan-berprestasi-inilah-enam-juara-pengusaha-muda-brilian-2024/ ;
   https://www.hipwee.com/feature/pemenang-pengusaha-muda-brilian-2022/
