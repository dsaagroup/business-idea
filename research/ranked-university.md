# Ranked Winners — University Programmes (Synthesised)

**Research date:** 15 November 2026
**Synthesised from:** `ranked-university-partA.md` (Innovative Academy + SkyStar), `ranked-university-partB.md` (BINUS + Greater Hub), `ranked-university-partC.md` (PRIME STeP + IPB), `ranked-university-partD.md` (UI Studentpreneurs + ITS Youth Technopreneur), `ranked-university-partE.md` (ATAVI + Bandung Techno Park) — plus the Part E agent's handoff report (see the Part E caveat below).
**Follow-up to:** `winners-university.md` and `cans/07-indonesia-programmes.md` (University section).
**Method:** `browser.search` (Indonesian + English) + `browser.open` on primary sources (university/organiser sites, Instagram, news, LinkedIn, press releases). Multiple sources checked per cycle. **No rank was invented.** Where a source names a Top N unranked, it is recorded as unranked — never converted into 1st/2nd/3rd.
**Cans files were NOT edited in this task** (research only).

## Rank-type vocabulary

| Rank type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ranked 1st/2nd/3rd` | Source states an ordered 1st, 2nd, 3rd (or Juara 1/2/3) |
| `best-N unranked` | Source names N best teams with NO order between them |
| `category winners` | Source names a winner per category (e.g. Best Pitch, Public Choice) — no overall 1st/2nd/3rd |
| `cohort / no-rank` | Programme publishes a selected/funded cohort; no ranking exists by design |
| `no-rank (showcase)` | Event was a showcase / VC meet-and-connect, not a judged contest |
| `no-winner-published` | Event happened, coverage exists, but no winner/ranking was published |
| `not yet held` / `in progress` | Cycle/event scheduled after the research date, or still running |
| `TBD-still` | Searched, not found; not proof no winner exists |

## Headline

Of the 10 university programmes, only **3 publish ranked 1st/2nd/3rd podiums of their own**: UI Studentpreneurs (BMCC, every edition), BINUS Incubator (via Startup Vaganza and Business Ignition events), and Bandung Techno Park (via KlikStart). The other 5 — Innovative Academy, PRIME STeP, IPB Business Incubation, ITS Youth Technopreneur, ATAVI — publish **no ranking at all** in any cycle: their published outcome is a selected/funded cohort.

## 4. The Greater Hub — SBM ITB

**Structure:** Business incubation programme held **twice a year** (two batches per year). Batches are **selection-based, not winner-based** — a batch's published outcome is the list of startups selected into incubation; the programme publishes **no 1st/2nd/3rd per batch**. Awards are given at the public pitching showcase, **Bandung Startup Pitching Day (BSPD)** — four category awards per event (The Most Innovative, The Best Pitch, The Most Impactful, The Most Marketable), no single overall winner. There is no published "Batch X winner". Programme scale (BNI Ventures partner post, Jul 2026): founded 2016 / operating since 2017; 408 startup alumni; 2,560+ UMKM participants; 20% of startups secured investment. Source: https://www.instagram.com/p/Da1tNJrAXYZ/
