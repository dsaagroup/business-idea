# Real Sector — Agri-food, Fisheries, Rural and Food Production

Research date: 2026-11-15. Scope: Group B of the real-sector expansion of the cans knowledge base (`~/workspace/business-idea`). Covers recurring (annual / repeating, not one-off) Indonesian programmes focused on agriculture, food production/processing, fisheries/aquaculture, plantations, livestock and rural enterprise — NOT pure tech startups. None of the programmes below is among the 47 already in `cans/07-indonesia-programmes.md`.

Method: 4 parallel research subagents (B1 government food/agri; B2 fisheries/aquaculture; B3 food-processing UMKM / corporate / commodities; B4 agribusiness incubators / rural / livestock). Each searched in Indonesian + English (official site, Instagram, news, LinkedIn) via `browser.search` / `browser.open`. Rank type per cycle: ranked 1st/2nd/3rd, category winners, cohort/no-rank, no-winner-published, or TBD-still. No winner, prize or rank is invented — gaps are marked TBD with what was searched. Conflicts are stated, not silently resolved.

## 2. YESS — Youth Entrepreneurship and Employment Support Services (countable cycle: Young Ambassador Agriculture, YAA)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Organiser | Kementan via BPPSDMP (PPIU: Polbangtan Bogor/Jabar, Polbangtan Malang/Jatim, SMK-PP Banjarbaru/Kalsel, Polbangtan Gowa/Sulsel), funded by IFAD. 6-year project 2019–2025 |
| Open to | Rural youth in 4 programme provinces: Jawa Barat (Bogor, Cianjur, Sukabumi, Subang, Tasikmalaya), Jawa Timur (Tulungagung, Pacitan, Pasuruan, Malang, Banyuwangi), Kalimantan Selatan (Hulu Sungai Selatan, Banjar, Tanah Laut, Tanah Bumbu), Sulawesi Selatan (Gowa, Maros, Bone, Bulukumba, Bantaeng) — 4 provinces, 19 districts (15 at launch, +4 in 2023). | Cycle | Fixed-term project, not an annual competition. Field implementation 2021–2024/25. Its countable annual cycle is Young Ambassador Agriculture (YAA), a selection + training of young farmer-entrepreneurs as YESS ambassadors, held every year |
