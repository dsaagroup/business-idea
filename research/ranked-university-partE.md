# Ranked Winners — Part E: ATAVI (UNAIR) & Bandung Techno Park Incubation (Tel-U)

**Research date:** 15 November 2026 (follow-up to `winners-university.md` §§9–10)
**Scope:** Fill TBD gaps and clarify 1st/2nd/3rd ranking for these 2 programmes only.
**Method:** `browser.search` (Indonesian + English) + `browser.open` on primary sources
(university sites, BTP landing pages, official Instagram). No rank was invented.
Where a cycle publishes no ranking, that is stated as **cohort / no-rank**, with evidence.
**Rate-limit caveat:** The browser service returned HTTP 429 repeatedly during this
pass. Three `browser.open` calls on Instagram posts (ATAVI 2025 Batch I, BTP Batch 23
×2) failed with 429 and were **not** retried; facts from those posts rest on the
search-result summaries of the same posts. One open succeeded (BTP Batch 22).
Items flagged "snippet/summary-only" should be re-opened in full before republication.

**Rank-type vocabulary used throughout:**
- `ranked 1st/2nd/3rd` — a judged podium was published.
- `category winners` — one winner (or 1st/2nd/3rd) per category, no single overall winner.
- `cohort / no-rank` — the programme publishes a selected cohort only; no ranking exists.
- `finalist, not winner` — external-competition finalist status, explicitly not a win.
- `no-winner-published` — a cycle happened and was reported, but no winner/rank was published.
- `TBD-still` — searched, not found; not proof it does not exist.

## 1. ATAVI — Universitas Airlangga (UNAIR), PASINBIS (ex-BPBRIN)

**Organiser:** Airlangga Startup and Innovation (ATAVI), Pusat Akselerasi Inovasi dan
Bisnis (PASINBIS), UNAIR. Previously under BPBRIN (Badan Pengembangan Bisnis Rintisan
dan Inkubator).
**Format:** 6-month incubation for UNAIR students, lecturers, staff and alumni with a
product/business already operating (≥3 months per Feb 2026 article; ≥6 months per the
Mar 2026 recruitment notice — discrepancy already flagged in §§9). Selection:
administrative screening → substantive evaluation → business presentation.
**Programme age — CORRECTED:** ATAVI did **not** start in 2024. UNAIR (29 Nov 2024)
reports ATAVI "mulai *running* dari 2018" and that it received **Peringkat A**
from Kementerian UMKM RI in the 2024 Sipensi ranking.
Source: https://unair.ac.id/atavi-bpbrin-unair-raih-peringkat-a-dari-kementrian-umkm/
