# Ranked Winners — Part D: UI Studentpreneurs & ITS Youth Technopreneur

**Research date:** 15 November 2026
**Scope:** Deep research to fill TBD gaps and clarify 1st/2nd/3rd ranking for two University programmes, following up `research/winners-university.md` §§6 and 8 and `cans/07-indonesia-programmes.md` (University section).
**Method:** `browser.search` + `browser.open` on primary sources (organiser/university sites, official Instagram, university news, founder CVs/portfolios). Searches in Indonesian and English. No rank invented. Where a cycle's winners could not be found, it is marked **TBD-still**, with what was searched.
**Rate-limit caveat:** The browser service returned HTTP 429 (per-minute quota) repeatedly during this session. Searches were paced ~60 s apart; some planned follow-ups were not run (listed in §5). Facts flagged "snippet-only" rest on search-result snippets from the cited primary domains and were not re-opened in full — re-open the source URL before citing in a published deliverable. Instagram winner names below rest on the search service's OCR/summary of the official @studentpreneurs carousel graphics (image text); the post URLs are given so the images can be checked directly.

**Rank-type vocabulary used throughout** (per task brief):
- **ranked 1st/2nd/3rd** — the programme publishes an ordered podium per cycle.
- **category winners** — winners per category/track, no single overall podium.
- **cohort/no-rank** — the programme publishes a selected/funded cohort and no ranking.
- **no-winner-published** — the cycle happened but no winner/ranking was published in any source found.
- **TBD-still** — not found in the searches completed; not proof no winner exists.

## 2. ITS Youth Technopreneur (IYT) — Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember

**Rank type: cohort/no-rank (funding-only) — confirmed, with evidence.** IYT publishes **no ranked 1st/2nd/3rd and no "best team" award** in any year 2022–2026. Evidence:
- The ITS official programme page (https://www.its.ac.id/careers/en/entrepreneurship/) describes IYT as: "an annual program for ITS students who run their own businesses, offering them the opportunity to receive funding support. Students whose business proposals pass the selection process will be granted funding from ITS… This program also includes a series of training sessions and regular monitoring and evaluation (Monev) activities, conducted over the course of one year." No ranking, podium, winner, or "best" language appears anywhere on the page. Format: teams of 4–5 students (1 leader + 3–4 members) from ≥2 departments, ≤7th semester, one stage (Early / Growth) and one of 7 categories (Food & Beverage; Cultivation; Creative Industry, Arts, Culture & Tourism; Services & Trade; Manufacturing & Applied Technology; Digital Business; Sociopreneurship). Each student may join only one entrepreneurship programme (IYT or P2MW).
- No search in this round or the prior round returned any "juara / pemenang / 1st / best" IYT result — every hit is a funding announcement, a registration call, or a founder CV listing "funded".
- **Therefore the 1st/2nd/3rd columns for IYT are "not published (programme is funding-only)", not TBD.** The TBD items are the **funded-team lists** (names + amounts), which ITS does not publish centrally.

**Organiser / channels:** Subdirectorate of Entrepreneurship and Career Development, Directorate of Student Affairs (DITMAWA ITS). Official Instagram is **@kewirausahaan_its** (Kewirausahaan ITS, ~5K followers) — the prior file's suggested "@its_ditmawa Instagram archive" is not the account that posts IYT; @kewirausahaan_its is. Contact on the ITS page: careersinfo@its.ac.id. Historical context: IYT 2019 — 10 junior teams × Rp10m, 10 senior teams × Rp20m, 18 funded teams after peer selection (https://www.its.ac.id/news/gali-potensi-pengusaha-muda-its-launching-iyt/).

**Do not confuse IYT with:** P2MW (national, Kemendikbudristek) and HETI Student Youngpreneurship (ADB-funded; growth-stage Rp25m). Several teams received more than one of these; only the IYT funding is recorded in the tables below. Examples kept separate: Upgrade UMKM — IYT 2024 Rp12.5m (early stage) vs HETI 2025 Rp25m (growth stage); Arsa Fajar Zamilin — IYT 2022 & 2023 Rp12m vs HETI 2024 Rp25m vs ADB "Awardee Start-up Inovatif" 2024 Rp95m; Jiryan Farokhi — IYT 2025 Rp15m vs HETI 2025 (amount not stated in portfolio); Anggita Maharani — IYT 2024 vs HETI 2025.
