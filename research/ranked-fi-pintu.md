## Rank types used

- **ranked 1st/2nd/3rd** — source orders 1st, 2nd, 3rd.
- **category winners** — source names winners per category, no overall order.
- **best-graduate group (unranked)** — source names a set of best graduates with no
  order among them. NOT 1st/2nd/3rd.
- **cohort / no-rank** — programme graduates/publishes a cohort; no winner named.
- **no-rank / no-competition** — programme states, or its structure shows, it is not
  a competition; participants/finalists only.
- **TBD-still** — no published list found; not proof none exists.

## 2. PINTU Incubator

Organiser: JF3, LAKON Indonesia, French Embassy. 4-month incubation, 39 sessions,
April–July, plus Paris Focus Week in October. Annual, 5th year 2026. (This PINTU is
the fashion incubator, NOT the crypto exchange Pintu.)

### Headline — confirmed NOT a competition

Evidence, re-checked this session against the live official programme page
(`pintuincubator.co.id/the-programs`, fetched in full 15 Nov 2026):

1. **No award, ranking, prize, "best designer", or winner category appears anywhere
   on the programme page.** The page describes selection/curation ("Progression is
   earned rather than automatic, with selected opportunities reflecting each
   Incubee's development and readiness") and the rewards are the JF3 runway,
   Première Classe Paris, and the Paris Focus Week — not a judged prize.
2. **Every brand on the page is labelled "Finalist" or "Incubees" — never "Winner".**
   Testimonials are headed e.g. "2024 & 2025 PINTU Incubator Finalist",
   "2025 PINTU Incubator Finalist", "2023 PINTU Incubator Finalist",
   "2022 Pintu Incubator Finalist", "2026 PINTU Incubator Finalist".
3. **Denim It Up "2025 winner" is a testimonial-only claim, and the same page
   contradicts a judged-award reading:** the quote reads *"Truly grateful to be a
   PINTU 2024 finalist and 2025 winner…"* — but the heading directly beneath it
   labels the brand **"2024 & 2025 PINTU Incubator Finalist"**, i.e. the programme's
   own label for Denim It Up in 2025 is Finalist, not Winner. No separate award
   announcement, award category, jury, or prize for Denim It Up (or anyone) was
   found in any cycle. Treat "2025 winner" as the brand's self-description in a
   testimonial, **not a confirmed judged award**. (Denim It Up article re-fetch
   was rate-limited this session; this assessment rests on the live programme-page
   fetch plus the prior research file.)
4. The prior research file records the official site's sentence *"The program is
   not a showcase, not a competition, and not a shortcut to visibility."* —
   **NOT re-found on the `the-programs` page in this session's live fetch**
   (the page text returned contains no such sentence; it may live on another
   site page, e.g. about/FAQ, or the page may have been revised). Do not quote it
   as verified-this-session; the no-competition conclusion stands on points 1–3
   above, which were verified live. **Independent corroboration found after the
   rate-limit recovery:** a 5-year PINTU retrospective states in Indonesian that
   PINTU "berbeda dari kompetensi atau fesyen show" — different from a competition
   or fashion show — "dirancang sebagai ekosistem kreatif jangka panjang" (designed
   as a long-term creative ecosystem), with 3 focuses: design/creative identity,
   business foundation and export readiness, cultural diplomacy through wastra and
   craftsmanship. Source: https://wanitaindonesia.co/spesial-5-tahun-pintu-incubator-inovasi-capaian-apreasiasi-talenta-muda-berkilau-di-kancah-global/
