# Ranked — Antler Residency Indonesia & Scale Up by Endeavor (Indonesia)

Research date: 15 November 2026. Scope: the last-5 cycles in `cans/07-indonesia-programmes.md`
(Incubators section) and `research/winners-incubators-accelerators.md`, plus older cycles
where they clarify a rank. Names copied exactly as published.

## Headline rule — read first

**Neither programme publishes a 1st / 2nd / 3rd.** Both are **cohort / no-rank**:

- **Antler:** no single winner by design. The "win" is a pre-seed investment decision
  (binary: invest or stop) per team at the end of the residency. Invested teams are
  listed unranked, never 1st/2nd/3rd.
- **Scale Up by Endeavor:** no single winner, no prize, no investment, non-dilutive
  5-month programme. The "win" is selection into / graduation from the cohort.
  Cohort companies are listed unranked, never 1st/2nd/3rd.

So in every table below, **Rank type = cohort / no-rank**, and **1st / 2nd / 3rd = n/a
(no ranking published)**. The substantive column is therefore the cohort / invested
list, not a podium. Do NOT present any cohort member as a 1st/2nd/3rd-place winner.
"Top 10, unranked" is used only where a source itself says Top 10 (Intura, Google AI
Solutions Lab — a separate programme, not an Antler rank).

  lists 6, and the SEA article says the round "culminated in the launch and funding
  of the 35 startups". 7-vs-6 and 37-vs-35 are Antler's own contradictions; do not

### Scale Up — rank evidence

- **No 1st/2nd/3rd in any batch.** Endeavor's own community page frames batches as
  counts of entrepreneurs/companies, never a podium: *"Our batch 1-9 comprises of
  127 entrepreneurs leading 67 most-promising companies."*
  Source: https://indonesia.endeavor.org/community-edit/
- **Batch 7 — why it stays TBD:** registration closed 14 Jun 2024 (newsletter);
  a Scale Up Academy session "for Batch 7" was held 13 Dec 2024 (Instagram reel).
  No kick-off/graduation carousel with a text company list was found in English or
  Indonesian search, and no Batch 7 participant has published a LinkedIn "Batch #7"
  honour in a text-searchable profile (unlike Batch 10, where LinkedIn filled the
  gap). The announcement, if any, appears to be image-only Instagram.
- **Batch 6 — why only 2:** Bababos is evidenced only by an Endeavor newsletter
  calling it *"an alumnus of Endeavor's Scale Up batch #6"* (Oct 2024); Bion Cycle
  only by a founder's LinkedIn headline *"Scale UP by Endeavor Batch #6"*.
  No Batch 6 kick-off/graduation list was found in text search.
- **Cumulative-count conflict — unresolved, do not use as a total:** the MainStory
  Batch 10 post says *"Since July 2021, Scale Up by Endeavor has supported: 10
  batches, 258 entrepreneurs across 138 companies"*, while Endeavor's community
  page says batches 1–9 total 127 entrepreneurs / 67 companies. 138 − 67 = 71
  companies cannot be Batch 10 alone (12 companies). One of the two figures counts
  something else (or is stale/erroneous); neither is used as a programme total here.
