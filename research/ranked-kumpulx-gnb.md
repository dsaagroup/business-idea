# Ranked findings — KUMPUL-X Accelerator & GnB Accelerator (Indonesia)

Research date: 15 November 2026. Deep-research pass on the two programmes in
`cans/07-indonesia-programmes.md` (Incubators and accelerators section) whose
cycles were all TBD in `research/winners-incubators-accelerators.md` (§9, §10).

## How to read this file

- **Rank type** is recorded explicitly for every cycle. Values used:
  - `ranked 1st/2nd/3rd` — the programme published a ranked winner list.
  - `category winners` — the programme named winners by category, not a 1-2-3 ranking.
  - `cohort / no-rank` — the programme publishes a cohort/invested list and no
    ranking; cohort members are **NOT winners**.
  - `no-winner-published` — a Demo Day or award event is evidenced but no winner
    was named in any source found.
  - `TBD-still` — no cohort or winner could be verified at all.
- **No rank was invented.** Where the programme publishes no ranking, the
  1st/2nd/3rd columns say so rather than guessing an order.
- Startup names are copied exactly as published in the cited source.
- "Prize" means a prize or investment stated for that cycle. A standard
  per-startup investment for all cohort members is **not** a winner's prize
  and is labelled as such.

## Verdict up front

- **KUMPUL-X Accelerator: 0 gaps filled.** No cohort name and no ranked

  competition, not a GnB win.

## 2. GnB Accelerator — Fenox Venture Capital + Infocom Corporation

**Programme (verified):** Joint programme of Fenox Venture Capital
(Silicon Valley) and Infocom Corporation (Japan), Jakarta. Launched
February 2016. PitchBook lists it as formed 2016, based in Jakarta
Selatan, now **Inactive / Out of Business**, 29 investments in total.
Launch plan (Digital News Asia, 24 Feb 2016): 16 startups, **US$50,000
each for a 10% stake**, mentors + office space, Demo Days in June and
December 2016. The plan's "remaining 10 in the second batch… in October
[2016]" did not happen on that schedule — actual Batch 2 was February 2017.
