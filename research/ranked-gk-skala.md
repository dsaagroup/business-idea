## Headline verdict

- **Confirmed 1st / 2nd / 3rd (programme-ranked): 0 cycles in either programme.**
- **Category winners: 0 cycles** (no judged categories published for either programme).
- **Cohort / no-rank: every completed cycle of both programmes.** Both programmes

## Naming collision — GK-Plug and Play (read first)

Two separate programmes share the name and must never be merged:

1. **GIA Jakarta** — Plug and Play APAC + Enterprise Singapore / IMDA.
   Singapore startups expanding to Indonesia. Numbered batches; Batch 12 Jun 2026,
   Batch 13 Sep 2026, Batch 14 Nov 2026. This is the programme in `cans/07`.
2. **GK-Plug and Play Indonesia (domestic)** — Gan Kapital + Plug and Play.
   Indonesian startups. Numbered batches ended Batch 8 (Jan 2021), then 2022
   vertical "Batch 1" cohorts (Fintech; Industry 4.0; Sustainability — see lead below).

  the Batch 7 post and @joinskala / @innofactory.id Instagram carousels.

  extracted by text search; several TBDs above (notably SKALA 2024/2025 and
  GIA Batch 11) may be resolvable only by a live-browser/image read.
- No rank was invented anywhere in this file. "Top 10, unranked" does not apply:
  neither programme publishes a ranked Top 10; cohort lists are unranked by design.
