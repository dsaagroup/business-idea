## 2. X-Camp (XL Axiata, now XLSMART)

Structural finding (confirmed again this round): **X-Camp is NOT an annual startup competition with numbered batches/winners. It is an IoT innovation lab** (built from early Nov 2018; inaugurated with the Ministers of Communication & Informatics and Industry). Its public "winner" cycles are the competitions/incubations run through or supported by X-Camp — mainly for students/madrasah pupils and XL Future Leaders awardees — not a yearly startup batch.
Rebrand: XL Axiata + Smartfren merged into **PT XLSMART Telecom Sejahtera Tbk (XLSMART)**, merger completed **16 Apr 2025**. X-Camp still operates as XLSMART's innovation lab (23rd floor, XL Smart office; co-innovation showcase with universities, Sep 2026 Instagram reel). Source for 2026 lab activity: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dc5UC2vKoOO/
