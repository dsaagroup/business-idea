Research date: 2026-11-15. Follow-up to `winners-corporate-bank.md` sections 9–10.
Question asked: for every cycle of both programmes, record rank type (ranked 1st/2nd/3rd,
category winners, cohort/no-rank, no-winner-published, or TBD-still), 1st, 2nd, 3rd
(or category winners / cohort names), prize if verified, source URL per cycle.
Do not invent a rank: unranked finalists are reported as unranked.

## Headline answers

- **SYNRGY Accelerator: 0 of 6 batches named a 1st/2nd/3rd or a single overall winner.**
  All six batches are cohort/no-rank (Demo Day graduates only). No cash prize was
  stated for any batch.
- **Gojek Xcelerate: 0 of 4 batches named a 1st/2nd/3rd.** Batch 2 is the only
  batch with any ranked outcome at all: an **unranked Top 2** (1Export, JioVio
  Healthcare) — no 1st vs 2nd distinction was published. The cross-cohort
  Xcellence (Jul 2020) named 3 unranked finalists (Etanee, Qlue, Jejak.in) and
  never named one overall winner in any source found.
- **Gaps filled in this run (4):**
  1. SYNRGY Batch 4 — 10 of the Top 40 names now verified (were 0).
  2. SYNRGY Batch 5 — the 7 Demo Day 2022 pitchers now verified (were 0 names),
     with an explicit warning that they are NOT the Batch 5 cohort (see below).
  3. **SYNRGY Batch 6 (2023) EXISTS** — the prior file said "no Batch 6 found".
     Demo Day 23 Nov 2023, 7 startups named, BCA total 79 graduates / 6 batches.
  4. Xcelerate Batch 2 — full 10-name list verified from e27 (was partial 5).
     The 10-vs-9 discrepancy is resolved as far as sources allow (see Batch 2).
- **Gaps still TBD (3):** SYNRGY Batch 4 remaining 30 of 40 names; SYNRGY Batch 5
  verified 6-name cohort list (names, not count — count 6 is verified); whether
  Xcellence ever produced a follow-up single-winner/collaboration announcement.

## Count conflicts found (do not paper over)

- SYNRGY graduate totals do not add up. BCA press, 26 Jan 2023: "five batches,
  graduating 72 startups". Summing the per-batch graduated figures in BCA's own
  releases — Batch 1: 8; Batch 2: 10 at Demo Day (11 selected); Batch 3: 10
  graduated (11 selected); Batch 4: 40; Batch 5: 6 (Sustainability Report 2022)
  — gives 74, not 72. Two graduates are unaccounted for in BCA's own totals.
  Do not use 72 to infer Batch 5 = 4. The Sustainability Report's "6 start-ups
  in the program" for Batch 5 is the authoritative count; the names are TBD.
- Batch 6 arithmetic is consistent: 79 graduates / 6 batches (BCA, Nov 2023)
  minus 72 / 5 batches = 7, matching the 7 startups named at Demo Day 2023.
- Batch 3: 11 selected at launch (incl. InvesProperti), 10 graduated at Demo Day
  30 Sep 2020 (InvesProperti absent from the graduation list). Prior file listed
  11 as the cohort; the graduated cohort is 10.
- Batch 2: 11 selected at launch (incl. Zero One), 10 at Virtual Demo Day
  1 Apr 2020 (Zero One absent).
