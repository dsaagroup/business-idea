# Winners — Last 5 Cycles: Group C University Programmes (Indonesia)

**Research date:** 15 November 2026
**Source file:** `cans/07-indonesia-programmes.md` (Group C — University programmes)
**Method:** `browser.search` + `browser.open` on primary sources (organiser/university sites, press releases, reputable news). No winner was invented. Where a cycle's winners could not be found, it is marked **TBD / Not found**, with what was searched. **TBD means "not found in the searches completed", not proof no winner exists.**
**Headline finding:** 8 of the 10 programmes are incubation/selection programmes that publish **no single overall winner per cycle** — the published outcome is a selected cohort (funded/graduated teams). Ranked winners exist regularly only for UI Studentpreneurs (BMCC), The Greater Hub's Bandung Startup Pitching Day, BINUS showcase events, and BTP's KlikStart feeder competition. Only two one-off "best team" sets were found for Innovative Academy (2023 Demo Day) and SkyStar Ventures (Batch 8, 2021).

**Rate-limit caveat:** During research the browser service returned HTTP 429 (per-minute quota) part-way through each subagent's searches. Facts flagged "snippet-only" rest on search-result snippets from the cited primary domains and were not re-opened in full. Those items should be re-verified by opening the source URL before citation in a published deliverable.

## 4. The Greater Hub — SBM ITB (School of Business and Management, Institut Teknologi Bandung)

**Structure — important:** The Greater Hub is a **business incubation programme held twice a year** (two batches per year). Batches are **selection-based, not winner-based**: a batch's published outcome is the list of startups selected into incubation. The programme's **awards** are given at its public pitching showcase, **Bandung Startup Pitching Day (BSPD)** — four category awards per event (The Most Innovative, The Best Pitch, The Most Impactful, The Most Marketable), with no single overall winner. There is no published "Batch X winner".

Batch numbering verified from sources:
- **Batch XIV** — opened 3 Feb 2023; 48 registered, **21 selected**. Source: SBM ITB newsletter (PDF): https://www.sbm.itb.ac.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/NL-2023-FINAL_RESIZE_compressed.pdf
