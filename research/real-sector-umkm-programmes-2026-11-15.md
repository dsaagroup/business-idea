# Real-Sector / UMKM / Social-Enterprise Programmes — Research Report
Researched 2026-11-15 (ID + EN searches: official sites, Instagram, news, LinkedIn-adjacent sources).
Rule applied: nothing invented; TBD where not found; conflicts named with both sources.

## Headline findings
- **CIMB Niaga "Kejar Mimpi" has NO entrepreneur/UMKM competition.** Its recurring competition, Kejar Mimpi Rising Start, is a **singing** competition (since 2023, 4 cycles: 2023–2026). Included anyway as verified recurring, with the correction flagged.
- **Yayasan Hadji Kalla has NO ranked entrepreneur competition.** Its recurring entrepreneur vehicle is **Desa Bangkit Sejahtera (DBS)** — continuous village empowerment since 2014/2015 (start-year conflict), cohort/no-rank, no published winners. "Kalla Preneur" appears only as a line in the 2026 programme plan; no cycles, cohorts or winners found.
- **INOTEK Foundation — UMKM untuk Indonesia (UUI) / IDEAL is the one verified recurring UMKM programme** with ranked winners: 2023 and 2024 ranked; 2025–2026 ran but published no ranked winners.
- **Fallback used: Ashoka Indonesia Fellowship** (Kalla fails the "ranked cycles" test). Recurring Fellow elections since 1983; 2022–2025 Indonesia Fellows verified (partial lists), 2021 TBD.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| Open to | Poor villages (≥30% poor population, per Kemenko Pemberdayaan Masyarakat data) in Kalla Group operating areas: South, West, Central and Southeast Sulawesi (+ Jakarta for Ramadan programmes). | Entrepreneur programme? | Partly. DBS is village economic empowerment incl. UMKM (e.g. JAMILA ginger products, Sambal Balacang). **"Kalla Preneur"** was named only once — in the 2026 work-plan presentation (26 Feb 2026) under Economic Care, "mendorong lahirnya wirausaha tangguh dan berkelanjutan". No 2023/2024/2025 mentions, no cohorts, no winners, no eligibility details found in ID+EN searches. **Do not treat Kalla Preneur as a 5-cycle programme.** |
