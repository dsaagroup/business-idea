## How to read this file

- **Rank type is stated per cycle, explicitly.** `cohort/no-rank` means the
  programme publishes selection only — there is no 1st/2nd/3rd, and none was
  invented. `category winners` means only named category awards exist.
  `no-winner-published` means the cycle happened (or the programme was
  active) but no ranked winner list was found in any source searched.
  `TBD-still` means the item was searched for and not found.
- **No rank was invented.** Where a source lists a Top 10/15/25 with no
  order, it is recorded as "Top N, unranked" — never as 1st/2nd/3rd.
- **Prize column:** none of the three programmes publishes a cash prize per
  cycle. SSI is non-dilutive with no cash funding; HUB.ID and 1000 Startup
  Digital publish no cash prize. "None verified" means no prize was stated
  in the sources for that cycle — not that no prize existed.
- **Verification limit:** this round hit upstream 429 rate-limits on
  `browser.search` (per-minute quota) and `browser.open` (all three direct
  opens failed 429). Items marked ⚠️ SNIPPET-ONLY rest on search-result
  text (in several cases corroborated by 2+ outlets in the same snippet set)
  and need a live-browser re-check of the cited URL before being treated as
  fully verified. Instagram-sourced items are marked as such.

## 3. HUB.ID Accelerator / HUB.ID Summit — Komdigi

Annual business-matchmaking accelerator + summit. **No single winner and
no cash prize is published in any year.** Outcome = startups selected for
mentoring + matchmaking, and startups named as securing investment /
partnerships. HUB.ID Summit is the matchmaking event (larger curated list
than the Accelerator cohort). From 2023 the Summit merged with Nexticorn's
summit (NextHub); in 2025 it ran as "HUB.ID Connection Hub" inside Tech in
Asia Conference; in 2026 as "HUB.ID Global Summit" inside Bali Tech Week.
