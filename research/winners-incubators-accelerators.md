# Winners — Incubators & Accelerators (Group D, Indonesia)

Research date: 15 November 2026. Source programme list: `cans/07-indonesia-programmes.md` (Incubators and accelerators section).

## How to read this file

- **Most of these programmes name no single winner.** Where no winner exists, the file records the cohort's notable graduates / funded startups and labels them **cohort graduates, NOT winners**. Do not present cohort members as winners.
- **TBD / Not found** means no published winner or cohort list could be verified in web search as of the research date — not that none exists. Search terms used are noted where a gap matters.
- Startup names are copied exactly as published in the cited source; spelling variants in sources are flagged.
- All 10 programmes covered. Cycles found vs TBD are summarised at the end.
