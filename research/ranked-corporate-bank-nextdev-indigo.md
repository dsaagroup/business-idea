## 1. NextDev (Telkomsel)

**Rank-type pattern by year — this is the key finding:** the award format changed year to year.
- 2022: **no Best of the Best at all** — top award was Academy Evangelists (2, unranked).
- 2023: **category winners (4 tracks, unranked across tracks)** + one Best of the Best chosen from the 4 + Favorite Startup. No 2nd/3rd.
- 2024: **Best of the Best + Runner Up only** — no 3rd place published. (Top 4 pitched; the other Top 4 names are not a 3rd place.)
- 2025: **full ranked 1st / 2nd / 3rd** — the only year in 2022–2026 with a verified 3rd place.
- 2026: **Best of the Best + Runner Up + one special-category award** — no 3rd place published.
- 2021 (fallback, verified earlier): category winners (5 tracks) + Best of the Best + Favorite Startup. No 2nd/3rd.

## 2. Indigo (Telkom Indonesia)

**Structural finding — Indigo does NOT publish ranked Demo Day winners at all.** It is a rolling incubation/acceleration programme (founded 2013; intake twice a year, ~6-month incubation), not an annual competition. Its Demo Day is an investor-pitching showcase: a cohort is presented, unranked, to VCs. No 1st/2nd/3rd has been published for any year found. This is now evidenced, not just "not found": the one fully documented Demo Day (2022) lists its Top 10 with no ranking and no winner announcement in any report.

### Rebrand — VERIFIED

- **15 Nov 2025, Yogyakarta:** Telkom launched Telkom AI Center of Excellence (AI CoE) and formally transformed Indigo — previously focused on startup incubation — into **AI Connect**, a centre for AI-talent collaboration and development, present in 9 cities. Announced by Direktur IT Digital Telkom Faizal Rochmad Djoemadi at the MoU signing with Universitas Gadjah Mada (Gedung GIK UGM).
- AI Connect is one of four AI CoE pillars: AI Campus, AI Playground, AI Hub, AI Connect. Per Tribe Leader Digital Product Patricia Eugene Gaspersz: "AI Connect merupakan transformasi dari Indigo Telkom yang kini kami fungsikan sebagai melting pot pentahelix dan sandbox inovasi AI."
- The name was already in use in Oct 2025: Telkom AI Connect Aceh hosted a 24-hour AI hackathon festival with USK on 24 Oct 2025, described in reports as "program Telkom AI Connect yang sebelumnya dikenal sebagai Indigo Telkom"; Telkom AI Connect Jogja launched 29 Oct 2025 ("Digistar Connect" event with Muhammadiyah).
- Sources: https://apacvision.com/?p=35972 ; https://www.tek.id/future/telkom-ai-connect-jogja-jadi-titik-temu-startup-lokal-b2nwf9tNd ; https://www.tek.id/future/telkom-usk-dorong-lahirnya-inovator-muda-lewat-ai-hackathon-b2nwa9tMB ; https://swarakepri.com/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/91774
- **Consequence:** there is no Indigo incubation cohort after 2025 in the old sense. The 2026 "cycle" is Telkom AI Connect (AI-talent / hackathon / collaboration hub, 9 cities), not a startup incubation batch. Indigo Game (game.indigo.id) still live as of the 2026 crawl — intake open year-round — but it is a separate game-studio track, not the main Indigo Demo Day cohort.

### Programme background (for rank-type context)

- Founded 2013 (as award event 2009; incubation from 2013). Rebranded 7 Sep 2021 from Indigo Creative Nation to Indigo, with twice-yearly batch intake + bootcamp. As of Sep 2021: 194 startups fostered, 91 alumni still running, 28 in programme, 75 failed; 52,276 digital talents via 17 IndigoSpace (ex-DILo). By 2023–2024 reports: 200+ startups incubated/accelerated.
- Funding model reported: up to IDR 2bn convertible note; early years US$18k–US$180k, no equity. Demo Days attract 200+ VCs (per secondary listings).
- Sources: https://www.telkom.co.id/sites/news-resources/en_US/news/indigo,-telkom%27s-startup-incubation-and-acceleration-program-rebranded-after-8-years-1445 ; https://startupindonesia.id/insight-center/daftar-program-inkubator--akselerator-startup-di-indonesia ; https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/indigo-incubator
