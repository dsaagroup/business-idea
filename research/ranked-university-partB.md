## 1. BINUS Incubator — Bina Nusantara University

- **BINUS Startup Summit** — annual Incubator event (2024 and 2025 editions exist on the Incubator news index); winners not found (see below).
- Campus-level events (BINUS @Malang, @Medan, @Bandung) and other BINUS units (B-Preneur Startpreneur, Startup Wars) are **separate** — listed only as related context, never as Incubator cycles.

## 2. The Greater Hub — SBM ITB

**Structure (confirmed):** Business incubation programme held **twice a year** (two batches per year). Batches are **selection-based, not winner-based** — a batch's published outcome is the list of startups selected into incubation; the programme publishes **no 1st/2nd/3rd per batch**. Awards are given at the public pitching showcase, **Bandung Startup Pitching Day (BSPD)** — four category awards per event (The Most Innovative, The Best Pitch, The Most Impactful, The Most Marketable), no single overall winner. There is no published "Batch X winner".
Programme scale (BNI Ventures partner post, Jul 2026): founded 2016 / operating since 2017; 408 startup alumni; 2,560+ UMKM participants; 20% of startups secured investment. Source: https://www.instagram.com/p/Da1tNJrAXYZ/

### 2D. BSPD Dec 2023 Jakarta — Best Pitch RESOLUTION ATTEMPT (page opened in full; still unresolved)

The SBM article was opened and read in full. Its award paragraph, verbatim:

> "In addition to Pet & Co, three other startups received awards: Econella for The Most Impactful Startup, Reason for The Most Innovative Startup, and Opportunity. Pet&Co for The Best Pitch."

**Why this does not cleanly resolve:**
1. The sentence names **four** awardees besides the headline (Econella, Reason, "Opportunity", Pet&Co) but there are only **four** categories in total, and Pet&Co already holds Most Marketable — so if Pet&Co also holds Best Pitch, "Opportunity" has no category; if "Opportunity" is the Best Pitch winner, then "Pet&Co for The Best Pitch" is a stray fragment.
2. The parallel structure ("Econella for…, Reason for…, Pet&Co for The Best Pitch") attaches Best Pitch to Pet&Co directly — the most defensible literal reading is **Pet&Co won Best Pitch as well as Most Marketable**. But the same sentence counts "three other startups" (Econella, Reason, Opportunity), which only reconciles if "Opportunity" won something unnamed.
3. "Opportunity" is most plausibly a garbled reference to **Peluang.co** — "peluang" is Indonesian for "opportunity", and Peluang.co was one of the 15 pitchers. But Peluang.co's category is never stated, and no second source names a Peluang.co award.
4. A further inconsistency: **Reason is not in Geekhunter's 15-pitcher list** (TITIK0, Antrique, Pateron, Nalar, Lectro, GoodFarm, MotoAMP, Pet&Co, Fycas, Ikanesia, summaverse, Peluang.co, Econella, DandosSkin, diklatkerja — exactly 15, no Reason), although Geekhunter's list is introduced with "seperti" (such as) and may be incomplete. The SBM article is therefore internally inconsistent in at least one place, so its Best Pitch line cannot be treated as clean evidence.

**Verdict:** Best Pitch Dec 2023 = **TBD-still / unresolved**. Lean (not citable): Pet&Co, per the direct "Pet&Co for The Best Pitch" wording — but only if a second source (event video, @thegreaterhub post, or a participant's own post) confirms it.
