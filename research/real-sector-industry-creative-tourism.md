# Real sector, industry, creative, craft, tourism and services — GROUP C

**Date:** 2026-11-15
**Purpose:** Expand the cans knowledge base beyond the 47 tech-dominant programmes. Find recurring (annual / repeating, not one-off) Indonesian programmes focused on real-sector manufacturing, industry/IKM, creative-economy production, craft, fashion production, tourism/hospitality, retail/trade and services — NOT pure tech startups.
**Method:** Three parallel research agents, Indonesian + English searches (official sites, Instagram, news, LinkedIn), `browser.search` + `browser.open` on returned URLs only. No `cans/` file edited.

Several follow-up searches hit upstream HTTP 429 rate limits and were not retried — those gaps are flagged, not treated as proof of absence.

## 1. Apresiasi Kreasi Indonesia (AKI)

- **Organiser:** Kemenparekraf/Baparekraf 2021–2024. 2025 teaser branded Kementerian Ekonomi Kreatif / Ekraf (ministry split from Kemenparekraf Oct 2024) — organiser for 2025 **TBD-still**, do not record as Kemenparekraf.

## 2. Anugerah Desa Wisata Indonesia (ADWI)

- **Organiser:** Kemenparekraf/Baparekraf 2021–2024; from late 2024 successor ministry Kementerian Pariwisata (Widiyanti Putri Wardhana presented ADWI 2024).
- **Open to:** desa wisata across Indonesia, registered via jadesta.kemenparekraf.go.id. Entrants: 2021: 1,831; 2022: 3,419; 2023: 4,573; 2024: 6,016.
- **Offers:** Trophy + cash per category (2021: Rp30m / 25m / 20m / 15m / 10m for Juara 1–5 in each category), national promotion, ministerial visitation. No mentoring/market-access programme found — it is an award, not an incubator.
- **Cycle/frequency:** Annual 2021–2024. **2025 did NOT run as ADWI** — succeeded by Wonderful Indonesia Awards (see below).

## 3. Indonesia Good Design Selection (IGDS)

- **Organiser:** Kemenperin, Ditjen IKMA. **NOT to be confused with Good Design Indonesia (GDI)** — GDI is Kemendag/IDDC, launched 2017 with Japan Institute of Design Promotion, 9th edition 2025, Design of the Year 2025 = Plus System by FORMAT. IGDS and GDI are separate programmes; do not import GDI winners into IGDS.
- **Open to:** Indonesian industrial designers (individuals) and industrial companies; finished products (Design Product) and concepts/prototypes (Design Concept).
- **Offers:** Certificate + right to use IGDS logo; trophy + cash for Grand Award / Best 3 / People's Choice (2021 total cash pool Rp400m; 2020: Grand Product Rp100m, Grand Concept Rp50m, Best 3 Rp25m each, People's Choice Rp25m); Grand/Best 3 in 2019 got a Taiwan Design Center workshop; 2021 winners facilitated to Golden Pin Design Award 2022.
- **Cycle/frequency:** Since 2001; 16th edition 2019, 18th edition 2021 (so 2019–2021 annual). Permenperin No. 26/2021: "diselenggarakan paling sedikit 1 kali dalam 2 tahun"; categories: Design Product — Grand Award, Best 3, Best 20, Good Design, People's Choice; Design Concept — Grand Award, Best 3.
- **Status problem:** No IGDS award edition found for 2022, 2023, 2024 or 2025. A Dec 2021 article anticipated IGDS in 2022, but no 2022 winners surfaced. A Kemenperin archive exhibition (Mar 2026) covers Upakarti + IGDS only for 2017–2020.

## 4. Penghargaan Upakarti

- **Organiser:** Kemenperin, Ditjen IKMA. Registration via upakarti.kemenperin.go.id.
- **Open to:** NOT IKM themselves as contestants in the usual sense — it rewards individuals, institutions/organisations and companies (incl. large companies and, in 2017, regional heads) who have developed/mentored IKM for ≥5 years. Categories in 2020–2024: Jasa Pengabdian (individuals/institutions) and Jasa Kepeloporan (medium/large companies partnering with IKM). 2017 also had Jasa Kepedulian (regional heads).
- **Offers:** Piagam + trophy + uang pembinaan Rp50m per recipient (2022 and 2024 confirmed; 2020 amount not verified).
- **Cycle/frequency:** Since 1985. Kemenperin (2024) says "setiap dua tahun sekali". Actual gaps contradict a strict biennial: 2017 → 2020 (3 years) → 2022 → 2024. A Jatengprov page says "setiap tahun sejak 1985" — contradicted by the minister's statement and the actual ceremony years; use biennial-with-gap.

## 5. One Village One Product (OVOP)

- **Organiser:** Kementerian Perindustrian (Kemenperin), Ditjen Industri Kecil, Menengah dan Aneka (Ditjen IKMA).
- **Open to:** IKM in a sentra IKM, proposed/registered by local government; legalitas sentra by Bupati/Walikota decree. 5 commodities: makanan-minuman, kain tenun, kain batik, anyaman, gerabah.
