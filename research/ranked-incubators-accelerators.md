# Ranked winners — Incubators & Accelerators (Group D, Indonesia)

Research date: 15 November 2026. Deep-research pass on the 10 Incubator & Accelerator programmes in `cans/07-indonesia-programmes.md`. Companion file: `research/winners-incubators-accelerators.md` (prior findings). Partial files from this pass: `ranked-antler-endeavor.md`, `ranked-gk-skala.md`, `ranked-block71-google.md`, `ranked-fi-pintu.md`, `ranked-kumpulx-gnb.md`.

## Headline

**Confirmed 1st/2nd/3rd in the programmes' own cycles: 1 cycle in the whole group** — and it is a hosted event, not a programme's own ranking: BLOCK71-hosted Startup World Cup Indonesia 2026 Demo Day (BANIQL / LingoTalk / Arummi Foods). No other programme in this group publishes a ranked 1st/2nd/3rd in any cycle. The only other ranked-ish group is Founder Institute Jakarta 6th batch, 2017: five best graduates, co-equal and **unranked among themselves**, IDR 100m Startup Launch Grant each — NOT 1st/2nd/3rd.

## Rank types used

- **Ranked 1st/2nd/3rd** — programme published an ordered winner list.
- **Category winners** — programme named winners per category, no overall order.
- **Best-graduate group (unranked)** — source names a set of best graduates with no order among them. NOT 1st/2nd/3rd.
- **Top 10, unranked** — programme named a standout list with no order.
- **Cohort / no-rank** — programme selects a cohort and publishes no ranking. Cohort members are NOT winners.
- **No-winner-published** — event held, no winner named in any source found.
- **No-rank / no-competition** — programme states, or its structure shows, it is not a competition.
- **TBD-still** — item not found; searches attempted are listed at the end. TBD means not found, not proof none exists.

**No rank was invented.** Top 10 unranked stays unranked. Only a published 1st counts as 1st; otherwise 2nd/3rd are TBD/not published. A standard per-startup investment for all cohort members is not a winner's prize and is labelled as such.
