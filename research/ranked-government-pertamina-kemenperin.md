## 3. PFpreneur — Pertamina Foundation

**Rank structure: COHORT-ONLY BY DESIGN. PFpreneur publishes NO ranked overall
winner and NO 1st/2nd/3rd in any year 2022–2026.** The "winners" each year are a
selected cohort (Top 350 / 153 / 100) who receive stimulan modal (capital stimulus)
and incubation via Pertamina UMK Academy. Individual cohort-member names are NOT
published in Pertamina Foundation press releases or news coverage found. The one
exception is 2020 (outside the window): Bananania is explicitly called "UMKM
terbaik PFpreneur tahun 2020" / "champion PFpreneur pada 2020".

**What the named UMKM actually are — do not conflate:**
- **PFpreneur cohort members** (Top 350/153/100): unnamed in press.
- **PFpreneur "champion" 2020:** Bananania only.
- **UMK Academy Champions:** a *separate, later* programme stage. PFpreneur alumni
  who go on to win UMK Academy categories (Go Modern / Go Digital / Go Online /
  Go Global / Go Green) are UMK Academy winners, NOT PFpreneur winners.
- **Pertapreneur Aggregator Top 10 / winners:** a *separate* Pertamina programme
  (launched 2022); its winners (e.g. Kainnesia, Pertapreneur Aggregator 2024) are
  NOT PFpreneur winners.
- **Inauguration-day appreciation awards** (e.g. Genah, Best Sustainable Product,
  PFpreneur 2026 inauguration): a single highlighted product award at the ceremony,
  not an overall PFpreneur ranking.
