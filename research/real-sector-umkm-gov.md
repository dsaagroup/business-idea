# Real Sector / UMKM — Group A: UMKM & Government Programmes

Research date: 2026-11-15. Scope: recurring (annual / repeating-batch) Indonesian
programmes focused on UMKM / micro-small-medium enterprises and general
entrepreneurship — NOT pure tech startups. Excluded: programmes already in cans
(the 47 listed in the task), loan-only products (KUR, BNI Wirausaha, BNI KUR),
and one-off events.

**Result: 11 programmes verified as recurring.** PLUT-KUMKM was investigated and
**excluded** — it is a walk-in service-centre network, not a cohort/competition
(see §12). YDBA UMKM BISA is its replacement. Pahlawan Digital UMKM ran only
2020/2022/2023 and appears to have ended; it is included because its 3 editions
are fully ranked and verifiable.

**Honest pattern:** most UMKM/government programmes are cohort/no-rank by design
(training, mentoring, expo, continuous mentoring). Only 4 of the 11 publish
ranked 1st/2nd/3rd winners in recent cycles: BRIlianpreneur (PMB),
Pahlawan Digital UMKM, WMP Berprestasi (Kemenpora), and Pertamina UMK Academy
(category champions, not a single 1/2/3 podium). No winner, prize or rank in
this report is invented — gaps are marked TBD with what was searched.

## 1. Entrepreneur Hub (E-Hub Terpadu) — Kementerian UMKM

- **Organiser:** Deputi Bidang Kewirausahaan, Kementerian Koperasi dan UKM (now
  Kementerian UMKM). Platform: ehub.kemenkopukm.go.id. Partners vary per city
  (universities, local government, banks, corporates — e.g. Telkom, SMBC
  Indonesia, Universitas Agung Podomoro).
- **Open to:** UMKM owners and student entrepreneurs with an existing business
  embryo; delivered as regional roadshows hosted by universities / local Dinas
  Koperasi & UKM. Not a single national application — each city edition selects
  its own participants (e.g. 150 selected students at UNMER Malang, 100 selected
  entrepreneurs for Entrepreneur Hub Jakarta, 75 offline + 20 online at Telkom
  Bogor 2025).
- **Offers:** Training/workshops, mentoring/coaching, business matching with
  investors/buyers/retail, mini-expo/showcase, access to financing (incl.
  Entrepreneur Hub Financial 2025 with SMBC on credit-readiness, curriculum,
  mentoring). Entrepreneur Hub Jakarta: 11 modules, 10 mentors, ~5 months,
  ending in meet-the-investor/networking.
- **Cycle/frequency:** Annual, as a series of regional editions each year (not
  batches). 2023: 21 cities/regencies in 15 provinces, ~2,000 student
  participants. 2025: rebranded as "E-Hub Terpadu" (integrated), 7,980
  beneficiaries in 10 provinces, 14,084 connected to the ecosystem; 2026 focus
  is integration into the SAPA UMKM superapp.
- **Launch-date conflict — picked neither:** a UIN Jakarta thesis states
  Entrepreneur Hub was "diluncurkan pertama kali pada 11 Agustus 2023";
  a Kompas column on kewirausahaan.id says 316 universities were involved in
  2022, growing to 371 in 2023. These may reflect a 2022 precursor vs the formal
  2023 launch — unverified, so no start year is asserted.

## 2. BRIlianpreneur / Pengusaha Muda BRILiaN (PMB) — Bank Rakyat Indonesia

- **Organiser:** PT Bank Rakyat Indonesia (Persero) Tbk. Two linked tracks:
  (a) **UMKM EXPO(RT) BRILIANPRENEUR** — annual curated expo + business matching
  (go-global stage); (b) **Pengusaha Muda BRILiaN (PMB)** — annual business
  accelerator for young entrepreneurs, awarded at the expo closing ceremony.
  Official sites: brilianpreneur.com, pengusahamudabrilian.com.
- **Open to:** (a) Expo: curated UMKM (BRI-assisted, merchants, alumni), in 5
  categories — Home Decor & Craft, Food & Beverage, Accessories & Beauty,
  Fashion & Wastra, Healthcare/Wellness. (b) PMB: young entrepreneurs; 2024:
  >2,000 applicants → Top 150 → Top 75 → Top 20 finalists across 8–10 regions;
  2025: >2,700 applicants, 8 regions, 15-week incubation.
- **Offers:** Expo: showcase/bazaar, business matching with international buyers,
  export deals. PMB: ~15-week curriculum (vision/mission, law & tax, leadership,
  export & acceleration, ESG), mentoring by Super Mentors (e.g. Billy Boen),
  prizes (PMB 2022: Rp200m/150m/100m for 1st/2nd/3rd + 0% loan; PMB 2024: total
  Rp390m + 0% subsidised loan; PMB 2023: "hundreds of millions" + 0% loan +
  6-month Majoo trial), retail/market matching for winners.
- **Cycle/frequency:** Annual since 2019. Expo 2019 (20–22 Dec 2019, JCC), 2020
  hybrid, 2021 full virtual, 2022 (14–18 Dec 2022, JCC), 2023 (7–10 Dec 2023,
  JCC), 2025 expo (30 Jan–2 Feb 2025, ICE BSD — 6th edition; note: no separate
  2024 expo, the Jan–Feb 2025 expo closed the PMB 2024 cycle). PMB's own
  numbering: PMB 2024 was called the "fourth year" and PMB 2025 the "fifth year"
  (BRI press release via InfoEkonomi/Young on Top), implying PMB began in 2021 —
  but see TBD below.
- **Minor count conflict — picked neither:** 2019 participants reported as 155
  UMKM (Kontan via TradingView) vs 115 UMKM (Poros Kalimantan); both agree on
  US$33.5m business-matching deals and 7 countries / 16 buyers.

## 3. Pertamina UMK Academy — PT Pertamina (Persero)

- **Organiser:** PT Pertamina (Persero), via VP CSR & SMEPP Management, as part of
  its TJSL (social & environmental responsibility) programme. Also called
  "New UMK Academy" from 2024 (format refresh; won Marketeers Editor's Choice
  2024).
- **Open to:** Pertamina's UMK mitra binaan only — participants must be registered
  in Pertamina's PUMK (Pendanaan Usaha Mikro Kecil) programme and pass curation;
  **not open to the general public**. 2024: >8,500 applied, 1,686 selected;
  2026: >1,500 in regional stage, 857 advanced to national stage (regional stage
  in 10 regions, 43 training sessions).
- **Offers:** Structured, tiered training + mentoring/coaching, webinars,
  exhibitions, market access (SMEXPO platform, Trade Expo Indonesia facilitation).
  Four development classes: Go Modern, Go Digital, Go Online, Go Global (plus
  Go Green cross-cutting). Grants, not cash prizes: production equipment (hibah
  alat teknologi tepat guna) — 2023: Rp30m per Champion, Rp20m per Runner-up
  per class; 2024: Rp660m total to 96 finalists + 16 champions; 2025: Rp900m
  total. Note: some participants also receive PUMK funding separately (e.g.
  Rp100m loan cited for Move Leather) — that is the loan programme, not the
  Academy prize.
- **Cycle/frequency:** Annual since 2020, ~5–6 months per cycle (typically
  Aug–Dec; 2024: Feb–Nov per one report vs 5–6 months per another; 2025:
  Feb–Nov; 2026: regional from Sep 2026, national until Dec 2026).
- **Cumulative scale:** 2020–2021: 750 graduates; 2022: 511 graduates (of 722);
  2023: 573 graduates (of 820); 2024: 519 graduates, 96 finalists; 2025: 581
  graduates, 100 finalists; total 2020–2023: 1,802 graduates; >2,353 by Dec 2024.

## 4. Rumah BUMN — Kementerian BUMN

- **Organiser:** Kementerian BUMN initiative, run locally by individual SOEs.
  Telkom Indonesia is the largest operator (its own network); other houses are
  run by Pertamina, BRI, etc. Initiated 2016; by end-2023, 231 Rumah BUMN units
  nationwide (Springer chapter, citing rumah-bumn.id/about).
- **Open to:** Any local UMKM (micro/small/medium, all sectors — culinary, craft,
  fashion, processed agriculture) in the house's region; join as "UMKM binaan"
  via the local house, not a national application round.
- **Offers:** Continuous (not cohort) training, mentoring, business-legality
  facilitation (NIB, P-IRT, Halal, BPOM), branding/digital marketing,
  marketplace onboarding, business-matching and financing access. Telkom
  structures progression as Go Modern → Go Digital → Go Online → Go Global.
  2024 Telkom figures: 5,647 NIBs, 2,127 P-IRT, 1,386 UMKM in Halalfest.
  No grants/prizes at national level. Recurring training series "UMKM Naik
  Kelas" (assessment via app "Naksir UMKM", 4 maturity classes) — e.g. Bandung,
  Feb 2025 (>170 UMKM) and Bali, 15 May 2025 (137 UMKM + 21 facilitators).
- **Cycle/frequency:** Continuous, year-round, per-house. **There is no national
  annual batch, cohort or ranked competition.** The only recurring competition
  found is local, not national.
- **Count conflict — picked neither:** Telkom-specific counts conflict by
  date/source: 42 Telkom houses / ~84,291–100,000 UMKM mentored in 2024 (Telkom
  via Hedra, 5 Nov 2026; Akupedia, 3 Dec 2025) vs 44 Telkom houses in a 2026
  Telkom post. A separate Telkom University thesis cites 248 active units
  nationwide. Use "231 by end-2023" as the dated national figure and "42 Telkom
  houses (2024–25)" for Telkom only.
