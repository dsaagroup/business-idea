English against primary sources where possible (organiser site, press release, reputable
news). Several `browser.search` / `browser.open` calls hit upstream 429 rate limits and
were not retried per hard-stop rules; those items are listed in Still TBD with that reason,
not silently dropped. Findings that rest on search snippets rather than full page reads
are flagged in the programme sections (notably Startup Weekend Bali 2019 ANGIN recap,
